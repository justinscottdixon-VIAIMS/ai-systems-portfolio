Edition-level history

Versions stay separate

Promos, commercial pressings and compilation excerpts can carry different artwork, durations and credits.

Artwork follows the original

Printed jackets, white sleeves and other packages use their own source evidence. Digital releases will not be depicted as vinyl without pressing evidence.
