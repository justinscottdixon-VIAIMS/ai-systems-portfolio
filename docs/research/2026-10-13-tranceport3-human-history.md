# Tranceport.3 studio recollections

Recorded 13 October 2026 from Justin Scott Dixon's firsthand account in this conversation. The owner explicitly requested that this human layer be included in the visitor-facing release history.

The three passages added to D003 are edited first-person paraphrases, attributed in the interface as “Justin Scott Dixon’s recollection of working with Sandra Collins, edited for this archive.” They describe recalling the club's energy in a controlled studio, shared understanding that sometimes needed no words, and listening from another room to notice details missed at the main listening position.

The source is oral history, not a printed album credit. The other-room example was also described more broadly for collaborative work. No named track, session date, room measurement, mono test or literal crowd simulation is asserted. The passages do not alter the separately recorded account of track-by-track construction in Pro Tools.
