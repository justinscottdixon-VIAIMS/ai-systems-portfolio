## GEO / LLM SEO implications

There is no single universal GEO index. Google/Bing crawling/indexing, AI-search retrieval/citations and model training are distinct. Removing a tab is not itself a penalty. Withholding its content gives search and AI systems fewer facts and supporting pages to retrieve and cite; it may limit relevance for career/release queries. This is an inference from the importance of accessible useful text, not a prediction of a particular ranking change.

Live HTTP checks on 2026-10-01 found: homepage 200; /archive/, /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml 404. The live homepage contains headings “External Nodes & Index” and “Publications, Credits & Releases.” Therefore, do not assume a player-only replacement preserves all current textual search signals. The unpublished archive has no currently live routes in this check; its absence is mainly deferred opportunity. Search Console/Bing index state was not accessed, so actual index coverage is unknown.

Recommendation: launch without unfinished Credentials, but first complete the small approved public introduction/contact surface and search essentials in orders 3–4. Later add the complete archive at stable URLs and update the sitemap. If any already-indexed URLs are discovered during index inspection, handle their removal or replacement individually with accurate HTTP status/redirect behavior; do not conceal unfinished content in hidden markup for crawlers.
