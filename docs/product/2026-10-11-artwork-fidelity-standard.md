# Artwork fidelity and clarity standard

Owner instruction, 2026-10-11: treat the site and all restored Credentials as an exacting restoration archive. Preserve design and history while making presentation clean and clear. This standard applies to future additions and review of already-restored artwork; it is not a claim that the entire site has passed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Verify every printed word, spelling, credit, catalogue mark and punctuation against source evidence or an explicitly owner-confirmed transcription. If any wording is undecipherable, ask the owner one image or text block at a time before reconstructing it. Plausible generated strokes are not acceptable lettering.
- Keep a source/confirmed-text ledger. Database-normalized names are supporting evidence, not replacements for the exact wording printed on a particular edition. Owner-confirmed text remains identified as such.
- Preserve layout, logo geometry, label/sleeve identity, intended colors and proportions. Retail, promo, test-pressing, CD/digital and vinyl presentations stay distinct.
- Remove wear under the owner's new-condition direction without altering intentional design elements. Preserve source originals and all catalogue/credit facts.
- Inspect the complete artwork at native resolution and close detail. Check the site card, full-art view and an enlarged detail where small print needs more display area. Thumbnail legibility cannot prove exact fine lettering.
- Record actual pixel dimensions and resolving detail. Upscaling and a larger file are not evidence of recovered detail. Recheck the final exported and served image; do not accept a sharp source if the final image becomes illegible.
- Keep all barcode digits exact. Do not claim authentic bar patterns or scannability from a generated approximation.
- Use an independent wording comparison for restored text before owner visual review. Do not classify small-print distortion as harmless merely because the overall cover looks clean.
- Approval of an earlier image remains recorded, but new quality checks may uncover necessary revisions. Present proposed replacements for owner review and preserve existing files. Do not silently substitute unapproved variants.
- Original capture stills remain evidence only and excluded from site presentation. No production publication is implied by local artwork approval.
