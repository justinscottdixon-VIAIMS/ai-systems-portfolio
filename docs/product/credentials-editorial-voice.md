## Owner-supplied voice reference — verbatim

**Capture the moments, capture candidly, the mix**
studio sessions, usually last minute a day or two prior deadline, the phone would ring,  its AK1200 and he needs to record a set for his latest release, a vinyl acetate showcase quickly captured using Pro Tools in a forward, timeline method, if the mix fell off, we'd simply do the mix over again, and stitch just before the bad mix, to the newly recorded mix after the attempt to redo was executed. Direct speaker monitoring via the TC electronics Finalizer Plus was used to simulate the end result of limiting and eq.

**Editing around the performance**
The goal was to be AK1200’s wingman and him not have to communicate was was needed, that was the style of working relationship we developed over the years. Dave was always drawn to being able to just do what he does because what he does happens on the moment, thats his brand, as soon as its contrived, he disowns it and never looks back. That was the world of DnB. Its the most cut the BS genre of Electronic music, and always will be. Unforgiving of posers.

**Preparing the handoff.**
Mastering? not necessary, almost taboo, why? because AK1200 played nothing but acetate's. Mastering was done at the lathe and DnB artist are fanatics with how their mastering is preserved. What was sent from us, bit for bit long form bounce stereo left and right master file on a data disc, burned from the edit session using ADAPTECH Toast. No master plug ins, no EQ no limiters. CD mastering would be performed in-house by the label and indexed at the duplication plant using the text file sent.
