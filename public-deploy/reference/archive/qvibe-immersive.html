QVibe: pacing and tone

The brief

Build music to accompany frequency-themed looping fractal visuals.

Tuning experiments

Dixon recalls manually tuning instruments around target tones, with a low-level sine-wave reference throughout each piece.

Breathing space

Long drones, sustaining piano chords and carefully paced openings and endings let the music breathe.

Unfolding into Atmos

Anxiety

Dixon selects this dark, textural piece as his leading immersive example. The Atmos version is artist-reported mastered and unpublished.

A stable foundation

He retained anchored source sounds and placed accompanying effects around the listener, considering stereo and binaural translation throughout.

Calibrated monitoring

Balancing bass management, LFE and the monitoring system made translation and dynamics central to the workflow.
